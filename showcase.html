How it works

One pipeline, every source.

1

Unified media

3D, image, SVG and video are all rendered into a single texture — no thousands of DOM text nodes.

2

GPU ASCII shader

A fullscreen pass samples luminance, depth and normals, then maps each cell to a glyph and a 3-stop gradient.
